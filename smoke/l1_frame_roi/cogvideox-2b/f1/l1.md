Primary: S_mom (momentum only) on the frame pool (leak-free): interaction rate (does the forecast predict a collision), then S_mom = 1 − (η_fc − η_floor) / (η_blind − η_floor) on gated clips with unequal masses, η = |m_A dv_A + m_B dv_B| / (|m_A dv_A| + |m_B dv_B|) pooled as a ratio of sums; 1 = as conserved as the probe can tell, 0 = no better than the mass-blind rule. Secondary: S_family (collision law: momentum AND restitution in [0, 1]). The ROI pool sits at the TRUE post-event location and leaks it (secondary). S_outcome and the r_p momentum residual are not reported (hidden-e ceiling; perfect for the no-event null). CoM / angular momentum: conserved by a forecast that ignores the collision, so reported, not primary (reference: shuffled-group forecast).
