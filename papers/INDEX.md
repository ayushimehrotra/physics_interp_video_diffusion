117 PDFs in this folder (all from arXiv): 50 for physics in video models (sections A–D) and 67 for benchmarks of model internals (last section). Two kept papers have no PDF here (see "Not downloaded").
Builds on the earlier review "Literature Review: Physical Laws in Video Models" (claude.ai artifact, 2026-11-05)
and `docs/references/` (cav, diffusion_interp, interpreting_physics, manifold_steering; the first three are
re-downloaded here under standard names, manifold_steering is not physics-specific and stays only there).

Columns: **Class** = encoder (self-supervised video encoder / predictor), generator (video diffusion / world model),
VLM (multimodal LLM), both. **Method** = VoE surprise, probing, generated-video eval, steering, post-training, other.
Key findings are from abstracts / papers as read on 2026-11-11; numbers for 2026 papers were checked against the
arXiv abstract or HTML, older ones are from memory of the papers and are kept qualitative where unsure.
